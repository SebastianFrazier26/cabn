# Sourdough loaf

## Starter

Feed 1:1:1 (starter:flour:water) the night before, leave on the counter.

## Dough

- 500g bread flour
- 350g water
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

Mix, rest 30 min, then four sets of stretch-and-folds 30 minutes apart. Bulk
ferment until ~50% risen, shape, cold retard overnight, bake at 475F in a
covered pot (20 min covered, 20 min uncovered).
