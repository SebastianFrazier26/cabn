Clearings sit much closer to their parents. The layout used to put every depth on one shared ring sized by the whole tree's leaf width and its biggest clearing, which left empty stretches of thousands of pixels between rings in big worlds. Each subtree now owns an angular wedge sized by what actually sits inside it, and each clearing sits as close to its parent as its wedge allows. Positions change, so worlds need rebuilding.
